# Code of conduct

Veltrix is built in the open, and we want everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests,
reviews and discussions) to feel welcome, regardless of background, identity, experience or
the tools they use.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to the project or to open source.
- Assume good intent, and ask before concluding that someone meant harm.
- Critique ideas and code, not people. Explain the reasoning behind a review comment.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and own your mistakes.
- Focus on what's best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments about anyone's identity or background
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome advances
- Threats, intimidation, or encouraging others to harass someone
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or repeated off-topic posting
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in every project space (the repository, its issues, pull requests and
discussions) and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer privately through
the contact details on [@goesq's GitHub profile](https://github.com/goesq). Reports are
handled confidentially, and nobody will face retaliation for reporting in good faith.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, code and issues that break this code, and
may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone who does, depending on how serious and
how repeated the behavior is.
